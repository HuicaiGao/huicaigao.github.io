About

Research at the intersection of data, people, and places.

Dr. Huicai Gao is an Assistant Professor at the Lee Shau Kee School of Business and Administration, Hong Kong Metropolitan University. She received her Ph.D. from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, where her thesis explored tourism demand forecasting using big data.
