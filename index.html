Short Bio

Dr. Huicai (Bella) Gao is an Assistant Professor at the Lee Shau Kee School of Business and Administration, Hong Kong Metropolitan University. Her research focuses on big data analytics and forecasting in business, with particular interests in tourism demand forecasting, machine learning, online consumer behavior, and multisource Internet data.

We are hiring

Postdoctoral Fellow / Research Associate

Full-time or Part-time

Applications are invited for the position of Postdoctoral Fellow / Research Associate to support a research project involving econometric modelling, data mining, and large-scale data analysis.

Major Duties and Responsibilities

Depending on the appointee’s specialization, the primary duties will fall under one of the following areas:

Data Analysis Track

  • Process and analyze large-scale multimodal social media data;
  • Conduct econometric modelling, data mining, and statistical analysis; and
  • Develop and maintain reproducible data-processing and analytical workflows.

Experimental Research Track

  • Design and conduct behavioral or marketing experiments;
  • Recruit participants and manage experimental procedures and data collection; and
  • Analyze experimental data and interpret research findings.

Both tracks will support the collaborative project between Hong Kong Metropolitan University and The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, prepare research reports, academic papers, and conference presentations, and perform other research-related duties assigned by the Project Investigator.

Candidates

Applicants should:

  • Be legally eligible to work in Hong Kong;
  • Possess a doctoral degree (for the Postdoctoral Fellow position) or a master’s degree or above (for the Research Associate position) in Economics, Marketing, Psychology, Information Systems, Data Science, or a related discipline;
  • For applicants specializing in econometrics, information systems, computer science, or data science: have sound knowledge of econometric models or data-mining techniques, together with strong coding skills in Python, R, Stata, SQL, or equivalent software;
  • For applicants specializing in psychology or marketing: have practical experience in experimental design and conducting behavioral or marketing experiments;
  • Have experience in preparing and publishing business / tourism papers in English-language academic journals; and
  • Have a good command of written and spoken English.

Appointment and Remuneration

The appointment period, working arrangement, and remuneration will be determined according to the appointee’s qualifications, experience, and project requirements.

Application

Interested applicants should submit the following documents by email:

  • A curriculum vitae (CV);
  • A list of completed courses together with academic transcript(s); and
  • Details of representative publications.
