Short Bio

Dr. Huicai Gao is an Assistant Professor at the Lee Shau Kee School of Business and Administration, Hong Kong Metropolitan University. Her research focuses on big data analytics and forecasting in business, with particular interests in tourism demand forecasting, machine learning, online consumer behavior, and multisource Internet data.

We are hiring

Postdoctoral Fellow / Research Associate

Full-time or Part-time

Applications are invited for the position of Postdoctoral Fellow / Research Associate to support a research project involving econometric modelling, data mining, and large-scale data analysis.

Major Duties and Responsibilities

  • Support a collaborative research project between the Lee Shau Kee School of Business and Administration, Hong Kong Metropolitan University, and the School of Hotel and Tourism Management, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University;
  • Process and analyze large-scale multimodal social media data;
  • Conduct econometric modelling, data mining, and statistical analysis;
  • Prepare research reports, academic papers, and conference presentations; and
  • Perform other research-related duties as assigned by the Project Investigator.

Candidates

Applicants should:

  • Be legally eligible to work in Hong Kong;
  • Possess a doctoral degree (for the Postdoctoral Fellow position) or a master’s degree or above (for the Research Associate position) in Economics, Psychology, Information Systems, Data Science, or a related discipline;
  • Have sound knowledge of econometric models and practical experience in data-mining techniques;
  • Be proficient in analytical or programming tools such as Python, R, Stata, SQL, or equivalent software;
  • Have experience in preparing and publishing business / tourism papers in English-language academic journals; and
  • Have a good command of written and spoken English.

Appointment and Remuneration

The appointment period, working arrangement, and remuneration will be determined according to the appointee’s qualifications, experience, and project requirements.

Application

Interested applicants should submit the following documents by email:

  • A curriculum vitae (CV);
  • A list of completed courses together with academic transcript(s); and
  • Details of representative publications.
Apply via hgao@hkmu.edu.hk

Research Interests

  • Tourism demand forecasting and business prediction
  • Big data analytics and business intelligence
  • Machine learning, deep learning, and ensemble learning
  • Online reviews, social media, and digital consumer behavior
  • Restaurant choice, recommendation, and survival analysis
